sprint-end: the facts a period ends on

At the end of a period each owner closes it from the record, not from
memory: the period's aggregate, each objective issue's ledger and reported
status, and the version the release should take. The sprint-end skill runs
this and merges the summary, which closes the sprint-end issue and the
objectives the owner reports complete.

Subcommands:
  tpm sprint-end facts   the aggregate, ledgers, and recommended version (srd038)

See: srd038, srd032
