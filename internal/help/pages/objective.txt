objective: the program's intent for a period, and its ledger

Goals, intents, and objectives are one concept here. An objective is written
once, as an objective document in the system repository, by the technical
program manager: what is wanted, why, and how its owner knows it is met. Its
items cite requirements by URL pinned to a release tag. Each owner then
receives a dated objective issue, and that issue is the ledger: planned
events from planning, evidence events from tests in a named environment, and
met. An objective is met by evidence, not by its issues closing.

Commands: tpm goals plan, tpm goals push, tpm plan push, tpm sprint-end facts

See: srd034, srd006, srd022, srd019
