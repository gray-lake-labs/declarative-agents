reports: weekly status, gathered from the tracker and asked only for judgment

Each person's weekly status is a report in the reports repository: the
facts come from the tracker, and the person adds only what it cannot know,
such as whether a Sprint Goal is on track. This group scaffolds that
repository, opens the weekly status issues, gathers a person's week,
validates reports, and aggregates them into signals by component. A cost
overrun has no signal of its own: report it as a blocker naming the spend
and the limit it passed, and the aggregate raises it with the other
blockers.

Subcommands:
  tpm reports init            scaffold a reports repository (srd018 R1)
  tpm reports status-issues   open next week's status issue per person (srd018)
  tpm reports week            a person's open Sprint Goals and activity (srd018)
  tpm reports validate        check reports against the format (srd018)
  tpm reports aggregate       aggregate reports into signals (srd018)

See: srd018
