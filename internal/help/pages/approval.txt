approval: a second person's read before a merge

No change merges on its author's word. Before tpm issue merge delivers a
branch, someone other than the issue's assignee approves the exact commit
it would merge with tpm issue approve; a commit made after the approval
needs a new one. A release of the system needs the same peer read through
tpm release approve.

Commands: tpm issue approve, tpm issue merge, tpm release approve

See: srd013, srd008, srd015
