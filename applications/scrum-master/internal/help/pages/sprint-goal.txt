sprint-goal: a team's commitment for a sprint, and its ledger

Each sprint every team receives one Sprint Goal, an issue in its own
repository that tasks files: the goal, the initiative items it serves cited
by URL pinned to the release tag, the environment where its evidence counts,
its steps, and an empty Sprint Backlog. At Sprint Planning the team lists on
it the stories it selected, ranked and sized in story points. The Sprint
Goal is the ledger: planned events from planning, evidence events anchored
to the story criterion each proves, and met. It is met by evidence, not by
its issues closing; until track-goals runs, the sprint-end merge closes it
as reported complete and records which stories are done.

Before release 04.0 a Sprint Goal was an objective issue; every command
reads one as a Sprint Goal, marker and ledger included.

Commands: tpm goals plan, tpm goals push, tpm plan backlog, tpm sprint-end facts, tpm sprint-end stories
