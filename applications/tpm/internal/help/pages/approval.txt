approval: a second person's read before a merge

A reviewer other than the issue's assignee reads the branch and approves
the exact commit it would merge with tpm issue approve; a commit made after
the approval needs a new one. Whether tpm issue merge waits for it is the
program's choice: with admission.require_approval in control/policy.md it
refuses without one; by default it merges without one and names the
approver when the tip carries an approval. A release of the system needs a
peer read through tpm release approve.

Commands: tpm issue approve, tpm issue merge, tpm release approve

See: srd013, srd008, srd015
