objective: the earlier name of the initiative and the Sprint Goal

Before release 04.0 a program's outcome document was an objective document
and each owner's per-period issue an objective issue. Both keep working:
an objective document under docs/specs/objectives/ is read as an
initiative, and an objective issue, its marker, and its ledger are read as
a Sprint Goal's. See tpm help sprint-goal and tpm help backlog.

Commands: tpm goals plan, tpm goals push, tpm sprint-end facts

See: srd034, srd045
